## Rule of Life in the Orthodox Church

*Typed from handout pages.*

The rule of life helps us to live the Christian life more intentionally.

It prevents us from drifting and allows us to think about the shape we would like our lives to have.

We follow the Rule to grow in holiness and to nurture our relationship with God through prayer, church, fasting, confession and almsgiving.

- **Prayer** - daily  
  Give God the greatest gift - your time and attention  
  Put all distractions aside  
  This is helped through fasting and confession make space for God
- **Church** - regularly - to meet with the family of God for worship.  
  Always dress as though you are meeting with someone really important and try to arrive a little early.  
  Learn all you can about the liturgy.  
  The more you know, the more you will understand and be able to teach others.
- **Fasting** - Wednesdays and Fridays.  
  Use the church calendar  
  Fasting should always be accompanied by prayer and almsgiving.
- **Confession** - Pascha, Christmas, other times
- **Alms** - for church and charity  
  Almsgiving is an important part of the spiritual life.  
  Those who have little money can give alms in other ways like prayer and acts of mercy.

### Confession

Pascha, Christmas, other times

Before confession one should attempt to recall all the sins which one has committed voluntary or involuntary - remember they are not to a man but to God and be specific.

> If we confess our sins, He is faithful and just to forgive us our sins and to cleanse us from all unrighteousness. (1 John 1:9) NKJV.
