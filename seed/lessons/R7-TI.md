## The Bishop’s Throne
Whilst explicating the function of the Bishop’s throne in a church building, attention should also be paid to the role of the bishop at large. In addition to the material provided for this week’s session, the letters of St Ignatius of Antioch are also useful to look at. Especially ch.8 of the following: [CHURCH FATHERS: Epistle to the Smyrnaeans (St. Ignatius)](https://www.newadvent.org/fathers/0109.htm)

## Vespers
In addition to the written material provided, one can elaborate upon the transition from darkness to light – how this plays out in John 1, and at Pascha – and how Vespers is a constant reminder of our expectation of the light to come – a light which we cannot fully dwell amongst just yet.

## Anointing Service
For this session, the teachers are free to develop the idea of how God acts through the Mysteries “psychosomatically” – that is, taking into account both the soul and the body, the immaterial and the material, at the same time. We are anointed with physical oil, and yet, our spiritual ailments can also be cured in addition to our physical ones. See also: James 5:13-20

## The Crusades
In addition to a general overview of the Crusades – including why they were called (in retaliation to the Muslim threat that had encroached even into parts of Spain), focus can be given to the third Crusade, which saw Cyprus taken from Byzantine rule by Richard the Lionheart, and the fourth (which involved the sacking of Constantinople and the severe damaging of its outer walls – an injury the Empire would never recover from).

## Saints Cyril and Methodius
This session will introduce the children to the Apostles to the Slavs. In addition to the material provided for this session, the following video with Fr. Nikita will provide some useful information on the missionary work, reach, vision and legacy of these saints: [New Beginnings with Fr Nikita](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=prrnhApV4vY&t=365s).

## Ezekiel
A useful structure, with provided materials, it found in the relevant folder for this month’s session. Reference to Ezekiel’s vision (Ezekiel 1) in comparison with how the four evangelists are often depicted can also be made if there is time. Images of these depictions can also be found in the folder for this session.

## Beatitudes
As well as reading the Sermon on the Mount (Matthew 5-7), teachers are encouraged to unpack the meaning behind each statement with the children. The following homily from St John Chrysostom will be useful in accomplishing this: [CHURCH FATHERS: Homily 15 on Matthew (Chrysostom)](https://www.newadvent.org/fathers/200115.htm).

## The Icon Corner
This session encourages the children to establish a “mini-church”, as it were, at home. It delineates what an icon corner is, and its role within our home, and our prayer life. As a useful initial prompt, the following should be consulted: [Icon corner - Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Icon_corner#:~:text=An%20icon%20corner%20is%20normally,(s)%20of%20the%20family.).
