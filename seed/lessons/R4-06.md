## The 10 Commandments

1. I am the Lord thy God; thou shalt have no other Gods before me.
2. Thou shalt not make unto thyself any graven image.
3. Thou shalt not take the name of the Lord thy God in vain.
4. Remember the Sabbath day and keep it holy.
5. Honour thy father and mother.
6. Thou shalt not kill.
7. Thou shalt not commit adultery.
8. Thou shalt not steal.
9. Thou shalt not bear false witness.
10. Thou shalt not covet.

## Moses and the Tabernacle

Moses went up the mountain to be near God to pray. He was so long on the mountain (40 days) that the Hebrews became impatient and Aaron, Moses' brother, helped them to make a golden calf to worship instead of God.

While Moses was on the mountain, God gave him the 10 Commandments. If the people followed these Commandments God would be with them. It was a covenant or contract, a promise. But Moses was so angry when he saw how quickly the Hebrews turned away from God and worshipped an idol, that he threw down the tablets of stone given to him by God, and smashed them.

Moses returned to the mountain to ask God for forgiveness and even offered to die for their sins and their lack of faith. However God allowed Moses to re-make the tablets. God also gave Moses instructions of how to build the Tabernacle: a portable holy temple for worshipping God, so that the Hebrews would know that God was among them as they travelled towards the Promised Land. He also gave instructions for their worship.

The Tabernacle was built of the highest quality materials using the best human skills. There was:

- An **Outer Court**, surrounded by posts and a curtain.
- A smaller **"tent"**. In this tent there was a veil or curtain that divided the **"Most Holy Place"** from the **"Holy Place"**. The curtain was made of linen dyed blue, purple, scarlet and white, embroidered with cherubim. Only the High Priest went into the **Most Holy Place**.

### The Most Holy Place

- This was where the **Ark of the Covenant** was kept. This was a beautiful box in which were the 10 Commandments; a golden jar of Manna (the bread which God gave the Hebrews to eat every day on their journey through the wilderness) and Aaron's rod, which budded miraculously.
- On the lid of the Ark were two golden Cherubim. It was known as the **"Mercy Seat"** because it was thought that God sat on the Ark between the Cherubim.
- Two poles went through rings so that the Ark could be carried when they continued their journey through the desert.

### The Holy Place

Only priests could go here. It had three pieces of furniture:

- The golden altar of incense.
- A golden 7 branched oil lamp.
- A golden table for the **Bread of the Presence**. On this table 12 fresh loaves of unleavened bread were placed every Sabbath (the day that they kept holy for God).

### The Outer Courtyard

- This had an altar for the sacrifice of burnt offerings.
- A basin for the priest to wash after offering sacrifices.

Every morning and every evening a perfect lamb was offered to God. It was burnt on the Altar. The fire of this altar was never allowed to go out.

The seven lamps in the Holy Place were lit at the time of the evening sacrifice and trimmed and filled with oil at the time of the morning sacrifice.

Incense was burnt in the Holy Place every morning and evening.

This was the way that the Hebrew people worshipped God.
