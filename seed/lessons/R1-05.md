## Questions

You can find accounts of the Nativity of Christ in the Bible: St Luke chapter 2, verses 1 to 20, and also St Matthew chapter 2, verses 1 to 12.

- Look at the icon. Who is the baby?
- Why did God come down to earth as a baby?
- Who is his Mother?
- What do the shepherds see?
- Who is the man sitting in the corner? Why does he look puzzled and confused?
- Who go to the cave to worship the Child?
- How did the wise men find the Child?
- Did they tell King Herod where to find the Christ Child?
- Why does the church celebrate this important feast?
