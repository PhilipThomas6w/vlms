## Questions

- After Jesus Christ’s Death and Resurrection the news about who He was and what had happened began to spread over the world. How did people hear this news?

## The Apostles start the Christian Churches

Just before Jesus Christ our Lord ascended to heaven He said, ***“All power is given to me in heaven and in earth. Go ye therefore, and teach all nations, baptising them in the name of the Father, and of the Son and of the Holy Spirit: Teaching them to observe all things whatsoever I have commanded you and, lo, I am with you always, even unto the end of the world. Amen.”*** (Matthew 28:18–20)

Ten days after Christ ascended to heaven, the Apostles with Mary, the Mother of God, were gathered in the upper room of their house. It was 9 o’clock in the morning and they were praying when suddenly there came a sound from heaven. A rushing of a mighty wind filled the house where they were. Then flames like tongues of fire appeared and rested on the head of each of the Apostles. They suddenly felt a new power in themselves: the power of the Holy Spirit.

From this moment they understood everything that the Lord Jesus had taught them and they were filled with enthusiasm and zeal. They were given courage and spiritual strength to face danger and suffering.

Suddenly they began to speak in many foreign languages so that all the crowds of people who had come to Jerusalem for the feast of Pentecost, and had been attracted to the house where this noisy wind was coming from, could understand everything that was being said in their own languages.

Peter preached his first sermon explaining who Christ was. His speech was so powerful that many were baptised.

Over the next weeks and months, the Apostles could not keep this new revelation to themselves. They talked about it in the Temple and in the synagogue, telling everyone that Jesus, whom they had crucified, was the one who had come to save us from death, and that if they believed that He was God they would have eternal life.

The Apostles even performed miracles in the name of Jesus Christ. Peter healed a man who had been lame from birth, much to everyone’s astonishment.

All this began to annoy the Jewish elders and priests of the Temple. The Apostles were causing a stir and it was not liked. They were imprisoned, but an angel let them out. St Stephen was stoned to death but he saw a vision of Christ in heaven and seemed happy to go to his Lord, the first martyr. Others were arrested and killed and it was not long before the Christians had to go into hiding and slip away.

St Paul himself was let down over the city walls of Damascus in a basket at night to escape being lynched for changing sides.

So the Apostles found themselves in other towns, escaping from Jerusalem. Everywhere they went they began to preach the good news, that Jesus Christ was the one they had been waiting for and that had been spoken about in the Scriptures. New communities started. Christianity spread unbelievably rapidly throughout the Roman Empire.

First a church was started by Peter, Barnabas and Paul in Antioch. This is where the name Christians was first given to those who followed the teaching of Christ. They spent a year in Antioch. Then Paul set off on his missionary journeys. He founded churches in Corinth, Athens, Thessaloniki and Rome.

Peter was the first Bishop in Rome. Mark went to Alexandria. Andrew went to Patras and Byzantium. John went to Ephesus. Barnabas went to Cyprus where St Lazarus was the first Bishop. Thomas went as far away as India. Simon the Zealot was martyred in Britain (York). James, the brother of the Lord, was the first Bishop in Jerusalem, where he was martyred.
