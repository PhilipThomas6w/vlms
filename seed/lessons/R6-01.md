## Memorials and Artoklasia

The Orthodox Church has traditions of praying at home while we are making food to take to church. We pray for someone when they have died while we make Kolyva, and we pray for the living while we make ‘Yorti’ bread for an Artoklasia.

### Mnemossina

We pray for the dead because although they are no longer alive with us, they are still alive in Christ. We pray for them just as we did before they died. We ask God to have mercy on them.

After someone has died memorial services are said on the 1st day, 3rd day, 9th day and the …………… day. Then they are remembered at 3 months, 6 months, 9 months and …………… months. Then every …………… on the anniversary of their death.

### Artoklasia

An Artoklasia is a thanksgiving service for the living. It is often celebrated on Name Days.

For the service you need five loaves of bread, olive oil, wine and wheat. These are brought as gifts to the church.

The five loaves remind us of Christ’s miracle of the Feeding of the Five Thousand when He blessed five loaves and it multiplied so that there was even plenty left over after everyone had eaten enough.

## Making bread for an Artoklasia

Making bread for an Artoklasia is a prayer of thanksgiving for the living.

**You need:**

- An icon
- A lamp or candle
- A censer, charcoal and incense
- Holy water
- Pen and paper for your list of people for whom you are praying

**Ingredients:**

| Ingredient | Amount |
| --- | --- |
| Plain white flour | 1 lb 4 oz |
| Sugar | 5 oz |
| Salt | 1 teaspoon |
| Cinnamon | ½ teaspoon |
| Dried yeast | 1 sachet |
| Warm water | ½ pint |
| Vegetable oil | 1 tablespoon, added to the water |

**Method:**

1. Mix all the dry ingredients thoroughly.
2. Stir in the warm water and the oil.
3. Lightly flour your work surface. Tip out the dough.
4. Knead the dough for about 10 minutes until smooth and elastic (add more water a little at a time if necessary).
5. Divide the dough into 5, with one piece larger than the others.
6. Shape into rounds.
7. Place in an oiled baking dish under a clean cloth.
8. Leave to rise in a warm place for about an hour.
9. Bake in a preheated oven at 375°F (190°C or gas mark 5) for about 20 minutes.
10. Place on a wire rack to cool.

**Take your Artoklasia loaves to church in good time with:**

- your list of names
- a bottle of communion wine (Mavrodaphne or Commandaria)
- a bottle of olive oil
- a container of wheat grains

Make sure the priest knows you want an Artoklasia (or “Yorti”) to pray for your family.
