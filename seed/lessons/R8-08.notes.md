## The Trinity and the Creed

*Lesson by Adain Hart.*

**Introduction:** Together we will explore what it means when we say that God is Trinity, Three in One.

We will look at three images that might help us to understand a little of what we mean when we say that God is Trinity: God is love, God is light, and an icon showing Abraham and Sarah meeting three angels.

### Love

a. We say God is love. How can God be love? I have one object there, representing one person. Can this person love without anyone else around?

b. No, so we need another person. We have two now, who can love one another.

c. Is love shared? Yes, so we need a third person to share in this love. We are going to look at the importance of three a bit later with an icon, called the Hospitality of Abraham.

### Light

a. In the Creed we talk of the Son being ‘light of light’. And the Holy Spirit descended on the disciples like tongues of fire. The Bible also talks of God as light.

b. We have three candles here. Can someone tell us how we can see these three flames as one light? You all suggested that one way is to line them up and look along the line so that they all appear as one single flame. You also said another way is to say that the flames are all the same substance but the candles that bear this flame are distinct.

c. So it depends how we look: from one angle God is One, at another angle He is three: Father, Son and Holy Spirit. In the Creed we say ‘I believe one God’, and elsewhere we talk of Jesus as ‘God from God, light from light’ and also of the Holy Spirit as the ‘Lord, the Giver of life’.
