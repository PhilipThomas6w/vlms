## The Resurrection

If Jesus Christ, our God, had not come down from Heaven to earth, had not become man, had not died on the Cross and had not risen from the dead, everyone would still be subjects of the devil. They would remain in darkness after death, for ever in hades.

The devil was a proud angel who thought he was as clever as God and could rule the world. He lied to Eve about the tree in the Garden so when the first man sinned, he said something like this to himself: "God doesn't know what He is talking about. Of course it will not matter if I eat the fruit from the tree in the middle of the Garden, the tree of the Knowledge of Good and Evil. I will be like God."

Ever since there has been a power struggle. Humans listen to the devil, or just think they can do exactly what they please, as if they are gods.

Luckily God, in His mercy, has decided to rescue 'man' from under the rule of the devil, and save him from the darkness of sin and death.

The Son of God, Jesus Christ, ***came down from Heaven and was incarnate by the Holy Spirit and the Virgin Mary and became Man, was crucified, suffered and was buried, and on the third day He rose again... And I look for the resurrection of the dead and of the life of the world to come*** (the Symbol of Faith, the Creed).

Our Lord, Jesus Christ, the Son of God taught His disciples what they needed to know about:

- Who He was;
- The Holy Trinity: God the Father, Son, and Holy Spirit;
- How they could escape and be saved from the power of the devil;
- How they could live a life in the Kingdom of God, and enter Eternal Life.

The disciples did not understand everything before the resurrection, while Jesus was with them, teaching and healing the sick. After He had been cruelly put to death and buried in the tomb, the women bearing myrrh found the tomb empty early on the Sunday morning, with only the grave clothes left there. An angel told them that Christ had risen and that they must tell the disciples that He would go before them to Galilee. Then they began to realise that Jesus was truly God.

That same morning Jesus appeared to Mary Magdalene, as she was weeping in the garden. At first Mary thought Jesus was the gardener.

He then appeared to Cleopas and Luke on the road to Emmaus and told them how the Scriptures (Old Testament) were full of sayings about Christ. The two disciples did not recognise Jesus Christ until He blessed and broke the bread at their meal.

In the evening He came to the disciples as they were gathered together in the upper room. He showed them His hands and feet. Thomas was not with them and said he would not believe unless he saw for himself, and was able to put his hands into the wounds and marks of the nails.

A week later The Lord did appear when Thomas was there. Thomas believed, saying "My Lord and my God."

The Disciples could not keep what they now knew to themselves but had to spread the News all over the world. We, too, pass it on to people we meet and to our children.

## Troparion for Pascha

Fill in the blanks, choosing words from this list: life, death, trampling, risen, tombs.

> Christ has …………… from the dead, …………………………… down death by …………… and on those in the …………… bestowing ……………

The troparion in full:

> Christ is Risen from the dead,\
> Trampling down death by death\
> and on those in the tombs\
> bestowing Life.

## The Resurrection in the Gospels

- The Myrrhbearing women find the tomb empty: Mark 16:1-8 (Matins Gospel 2); Luke 24:1-12 (Matins Gospel 4).
- Christ appears to Mary Magdalene: Mark 16:9-20 (Matins Gospel 3).
- Jesus appears to two Disciples on the road to Emmaus: Luke 24:13-35 (Matins Gospel 5).
- Thomas believes that Christ is God: John 20:19-31 (Matins Gospel 9).

## The Resurrection of Christ

"Christ is Risen!" With those words we announce the greatest event in the history of our world and in our faith. Jesus Christ, the Son of God, who came to save us all, died on a cross. But three days later, he burst forth from the tomb, alive! This seems so incredible, but it shows the power of God's love. See St Matthew 28:1-10.
