## The Icon Corner and Prayers at Home

An icon corner is the place in the home where we can focus on God and say our prayers. It looks like a mini church as it will always have an icon of Christ and the Mother of God, other icons of saints, especially the saints that are the patrons of family members. Sometimes there will be icons of the feasts.

It will be where the family say prayers together, so it will need to have other things nearby.

Things you may find in an icon corner:

- Icon of Christ
- Icon of the Mother of God
- Icons of saints family members are named after
- Incense and incense burner, charcoal, incense
- An oil lamp or night lights
- Sand tray and candles
- Holy water
- Holy oil
- Prayer book
- Bible
- Church calendar
- Prosphora seal
- Photos of close family who have died

The icon corner is where we say prayers but sometimes we hear of people who urgently need prayers, so we can go to the icon corner and light a special candle for them, while asking for God’s mercy. The light also reminds us that God is present.

We can keep holy things in our icon corner. It is where we can find the holy water if we need it. It is where we can keep our prosphora seal, and the calendar which tells us who the saints of the day are and what the fasting is for the day.

We always start praying by making the sign of the cross, venerating the icons and then we say the Trisagion Prayers. After this you choose appropriate other prayers. There are many beautiful Orthodox prayers. You will find them at the beginning of a prayer book, but it is good to know them by heart as you may need them anytime, anywhere. It is a good thing to pray for other people too. Many people have their own icons in their bedroom.
