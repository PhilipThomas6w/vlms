## Questions

- What does the word ‘Gospel’ (‘Evangelion’) mean?
- Where is the Gospel book kept in Church?
- What are the icons on the Gospel book?
- Who and what does the Gospel tell us about?

## The Bible, The Gospel Book

The words “Gospel” or “Evangelion” mean Good News. The Good News is about Jesus, who tells us that He is the Son of God.

He came from heaven to earth to save us from everlasting death so that we can live with Him in heaven for ever.

We know Jesus Christ through reading the Gospels.

We give the Gospel Book the respect we give Christ. It is kept on the Holy Altar. The Priest makes the sign of the Cross with the Gospel Book over the Altar at the beginning of the Liturgy as he says “Blessed is the Kingdom of the Father and of the Son and of the Holy Spirit, both now and ever and unto ages of ages!”

- We stand.
- We bow.
- We make the sign of the cross.
- We kiss the Gospel Book.

We sign ourselves with the sign of the cross to ask God to help us understand His Word.

The Priest, or Deacon, reads the Holy Gospel. It is the word of God. Jesus is speaking to us, to enlighten the World. Listening to the Gospel is one way we learn about God. This is why candles always accompany the Gospel. They remind us that we are listening to God.

The Priest blesses us with the Holy Gospel.

![Line drawing of the cover of the Gospel Book: Christ in the centre within a pointed oval, with the four evangelists in the corners, St Matthew and St Mark above and St John and St Luke below, each named beside his portrait](gospel-book-cover.jpg)

The icons that are usually found on the Gospel Book are the Resurrection on one side and the Crucifixion on the other side. Icons of Matthew, Mark, Luke and John are on four corners because they wrote the gospels.

We sometimes venerate the Gospel Book as Jesus Christ is present in it as the Word of God, the Logos.

The Priest or Deacon carries the Gospel Book around the Church in a procession with candles during the Little Entrance.

![Line drawing of the Little Entrance: a priest in a vestment patterned with crosses carries the Gospel Book raised in front of him, with a young server beside him holding a candle](little-entrance.jpg)

When the Priest or Deacon reads from the Gospel Book we should always stand because we know it is Jesus Christ speaking to us through His Word.

![Line drawing of the reading of the Gospel: a priest reads from the Gospel Book on a stand with a cross on its front, in front of the icon screen, with a server holding a candle on each side; the words beneath the drawing repeat that the Priest reads the Holy Gospel, we make the sign of the cross, the Priest blesses us with the Gospel and candles accompany it](reading-the-gospel.jpg)
