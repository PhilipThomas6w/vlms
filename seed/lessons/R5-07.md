## Meeting of the Lord

2nd February. Luke 2:22–40.

Forty days after Jesus was born Mary and Joseph took him up to the Temple as was the custom. The prophet Symeon recognised who Jesus was as did Anna.

## The Raising of Lazarus

The Saturday before Palm Sunday. John 11:1–45.

Jesus had a message from the sisters of Lazarus to say that His good friend was very ill. By the time Jesus reached Bethany Lazarus had already been dead for four days. Martha met Jesus on His way. She went to tell Mary that He had arrived. Both sisters believed that Jesus could have saved Lazarus had He been there sooner. However, Jesus had deliberately delayed so that a greater miracle could happen. Although the people insisted Lazarus’s body would stink, when Jesus arrived at the tomb He called out “Lazarus, come out”. Lazarus came out of the grave still bound in his grave clothes but alive. Many believed.

## The Entry into Jerusalem – Palm Sunday

The Sunday before Pascha. Mark 11:1–11.

This is the feast of the triumphal entrance of Jesus into Jerusalem on a white colt (donkey). They greeted Him as an earthly king. The people of Jerusalem laid a path of clothes and palm leaves in front of Him and shouted “Hosannah”. They were welcoming Him as king, but He enters as the King of Heaven.

## The Transfiguration

6th of August. Matthew 17:1–9.

The people could accept Christ as a man. They looked to Him as an earthly king. But they found it hard to follow His teachings about humility, one that He would demonstrate by His own death on the Cross.

Therefore, in order to strengthen their faith, Christ led three chosen disciples, Peter, James and John up a high mountain and showed them His glory as God. He was transfigured before them. His face shone as the sun and His garments were as white as light. Moses and Elias appeared talking to Him. The disciples were overcome with fear and fell down in terror at the revelation. Peter was soon on his feet again worshipping God.

![Icon of the Transfiguration: Christ in white in a circle of light on the mountain, with Elias and Moses on either side and Peter, James and John fallen to the ground below](transfiguration.jpg)

## Ascension

Acts of the Apostles 1:1–12. Matthew 28:18–20.

Jesus ascended to heaven 40 days after His resurrection. He took the disciples to the Mount of Olives, blessed them and told them to make disciples of all nations, baptising them in the name of the Father, and of the Son and of the Holy Spirit, and teaching them to observe all things that he had commanded them. He told them he would be always with them to the end of the age. The Apostles were then told to go to Jerusalem and wait for the Holy Spirit to come.
