## The Nativity of the Mother of God (8th September)

The story of this feast is a Tradition which comes from one of the earliest Christian writings called the Protoevangelion of James. There were a number of books written by Christians which the Church did not select to be part of the Bible when it was decided what was most useful to read in Church.

Mary's parents, Joachim and Anna, who we remember at every service, were becoming elderly and were very sad that they had no children. At that time it was considered a disgrace if God didn't bless a couple with a family. They were very devout, remembering to pray and worship God and they always did what they could to help the poor.

One day Joachim was thrown out of the Temple because he had no children so he was thought not to be worthy to be there. In his sadness he went out into the countryside to pray.

While he was away Anna was also fervently praying when an angel came to her telling her she would indeed have a child and that she must go to meet Joachim at the city gate. At the same time Joachim came to meet Anna. They were overjoyed that God had heard their prayer and not forgotten them. They promised to dedicate their child to God. Their child was Mary.

![Icon of the Nativity of the Mother of God, with the title in Greek: Anna lies on a bed under a canopy while women attend her, one bathing the newborn Mary in a basin and another holding her, with Joachim looking in at a doorway](nativity-of-the-mother-of-god.jpg)

### Troparion of the Nativity of the Mother of God

> Thy Nativity, O Virgin,\
> Has brought joy to all the world.\
> The Son of Righteousness, Christ our God,\
> Has shone forth from thee, O Theotokos.\
> By annulling the curse, He bestowed a blessing,\
> By destroying death, He has granted us eternal life.

## The Entry of the Mother of God into the Temple (21st November)

This is another Tradition of the Church which we have from the Protoevangelion of James, an ancient writing other than the Bible.

When Mary was three years old Joachim and Anna took her to the Temple. They were getting old and they knew she would be looked after there and would learn the hymns and services and be involved with the daily life of the Temple. It was as if Mary was to live in what we think of as a monastery today.

Joachim and Anna were afraid that their daughter would be shy and not want to stay in the Temple so they took some young girls along with them who carried candles to encourage Mary. However, when they were met by Zacharias, the High Priest, she skipped along and up the steps.

Mary lived in the Temple until she was a young woman. She was then betrothed (engaged) to Joseph, a very devout man, who already had several children but his wife had died.

We never forget that Mary became the Temple of God because God the Son, Jesus Christ, dwelt in her womb.

![Icon of the Entry of the Mother of God into the Temple: the child Mary, in a dark robe, is received by the High Priest at the Temple steps, led by her parents Joachim and Anna and by young women with candles, while above her an angel brings her bread and she is shown seated within the Temple](entry-into-the-temple.jpg)

### Kontakion of the Entry

> The Most Pure Temple of the Saviour,\
> Today enters into the house of the Lord.\
> She brings with her the grace of the Holy Spirit.\
> And the Angels of God sing of her:\
> This is the True and living Temple.

## The Annunciation (25th March)

Mary says 'Yes'.

### Gospel according to Luke 1:26-38

> Now in the sixth month the angel Gabriel was sent by God to a city of Galilee named Nazareth, to a virgin betrothed to a man whose name was Joseph, of the house of David. The virgin’s name was Mary. And having come in, the angel said to her, “Rejoice, highly favored one, the Lord is with you; blessed are you among women!”
>
> But when she saw him, she was troubled at his saying, and considered what manner of greeting this was. Then the angel said to her, “Do not be afraid, Mary, for you have found favor with God. And behold, you will conceive in your womb and bring forth a Son, and shall call His name JESUS. He will be great, and will be called the Son of the Highest; and the Lord God will give Him the throne of His father David. And He will reign over the house of Jacob forever, and of His kingdom there will be no end.”
>
> Then Mary said to the angel, “How can this be, since I do not know a man?”
>
> And the angel answered and said to her, “The Holy Spirit will come upon you, and the power of the Highest will overshadow you; therefore, also, that Holy One who is to be born will be called the Son of God. Now indeed, Elizabeth your relative has also conceived a son in her old age; and this is now the sixth month for her who was called barren. For with God nothing will be impossible.”
>
> Then Mary said, “Behold the maidservant of the Lord! Let it be to me according to your word.” And the angel departed from her. (Luke 1:26-38) NKJV.

### Troparion of the Annunciation

> Today is the beginning of our salvation,\
> The Son of God becomes the Son of the Virgin\
> As Gabriel announces the coming of Grace.\
> Together with him let us cry to the Theotokos:\
> Rejoice (Hail), O Full of Grace,\
> The Lord is with Thee!

## The Dormition of the Mother of God (15th August)

The Church Tradition of this feast is that the Mother of God knew she was to die soon so she summoned all the Disciples to her home in Jerusalem. They came from far and near to be with her, some even arriving miraculously through the air. She blessed them and prayed for them, reminding them of their importance as well as hers.

The Disciples buried her with love and sadness but knew that 'All generations would call her Blessed'.

Thomas, who had taken the Christian message to India, arrived three days too late for the funeral. However, the disciples took him to the tomb of the Mother of God so that he could give her his last kiss.

When they opened the grave they found that it was empty! Her Son, Jesus Christ, had already taken His Mother's body, as well as her soul to heaven.

![Icon of the Dormition of the Mother of God, with the title in Greek: the Mother of God lies on a bier surrounded by the Apostles and bishops, while Christ stands behind her holding her soul as a small swaddled figure, with angels beside him](dormition.jpg)

### Troparion of the Dormition

> In giving birth thou didst preserve thy virginity,\
> In falling asleep thou didst not forsake the world, O Theotokos.\
> Thou wast translated to Life, O Mother of Life,\
> And by thy prayers thou dost deliver our souls from death.
