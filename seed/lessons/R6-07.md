## Parables

A Parable is a short story which is easy to remember, used to reveal an important fact. The Gospels are full of parables - stories that Christ told to teach about the Kingdom of God. For us the Kingdom of God is a puzzle or a Mystery, but by knowing the stories we can understand better what the reality is and how we can be part of this Kingdom. Christ used every-day events to explain Himself so that those who bother to work out the spiritual meaning can begin to understand.

### Six parables

1. The Good Samaritan: Luke 10:25-37
2. The Prodigal Son: Luke 15:11-32
3. The Publican and the Pharisee: Luke 18:9-14
4. The Parable of the Hypocrite: Matthew 7:1-5
5. The Labourers in the Vineyard: Matthew 20:1-16
6. The Parable of the Rich Man and Lazarus: Luke 16:19-31
