## The Old Testament: Creation

On the first day God said let there be light. He called the light ‘day’. He called the darkness night.

On the second day God said let there be sky above the water.

On the third day God separated water from the dry land. He called the land ‘earth’ and the water ‘sea’. Then God said let all kinds of plants grow on the earth.

On the fourth day God made the sun to give the earth light by day and the moon and stars to give light by night.

On the fifth day God made the fish and all the creatures that swim in the sea. He filled the sky with all kinds of birds.

On the sixth day God made the animals, small and big. He also made man and woman. The man he called ‘Adam’ and the woman ‘Eve’. He made according to His likeness.

God saw that all He made was good. *On the seventh day God rested.*

Adam and Eve were told by God that they could go anywhere in the garden that had been made for them and they could eat whatever fruit they liked. But they must not eat the fruit from the tree of ‘the knowledge of good and evil’ that was in the middle of the garden.

One day the serpent encouraged Eve to disobey God. He told her to eat the forbidden fruit. She did so, and then she gave some to Adam to eat. After they had been disobedient, they were so ashamed that they tried to hide from God. When God came looking for Adam and Eve they were not sorry for what they had done. Adam blamed Eve and Eve blamed the serpent.

They were shut out of the Garden of Paradise and were told that from now on they would have to plough and sow and harvest, in fact work very hard to live. God said that they would die rather than go on sinning and suffering for ever and ever.

## The Old Testament: Noah and the Great Flood

*Genesis, from chapter 6*

One day God talked to Noah. He said: ‘There is going to be a flood over the earth because most people have forgotten Me. They cheat and lie they fight and steal and even kill each other. You and your family are good people and I want you to survive the flood. You must build an enormous ark and take onto it a male and female of every living creature.’

Noah and his family set to work to build the ark. All his friends and neighbours laughed at him. He tried to tell people what was going to happen but they did not believe him. He tried to tell them to follow God and then they would be saved too, but they would not listen to him.

When Noah finished the ark, he and his sons collected all the animals and brought them into the ark. It was like a great floating zoo.

When Noah had finished, it started to rain. He and his family ran into the ark and shut the door.

It rained hard for forty days. Soon the ark was floating on the water. Noah and his family were kept busy looking after all the animals.

After some time, Noah wondered if the earth was now dry enough to leave the ark. He let a raven fly out, but it didn’t come back. Noah then let a dove out of the ark. She brought back an olive leaf. Noah knew that the water was going down at last. The next time he let the dove out, she did not come back, so he knew it was time for everyone to get out onto dry land again.

Noah worshipped God when he came out of the ark. God said that there would never be a huge flood like that again, and put a rainbow in the sky so that we would remember His promise.
