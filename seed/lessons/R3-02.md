## Questions

- The first four of the Ten Commandments tell us to make God number one in our life. What are the next six Commandments about?
- What story did Jesus tell the man who asked Him how to gain eternal life?

## The Ten Commandments

The first four Commandments are about our relationship with God.

1. I am the Lord thy God; thou shalt have no other Gods before me.
2. Thou shalt not make unto thyself any graven image.
3. Thou shalt not take the name of the Lord thy God in vain.
4. Remember the Sabbath day and keep it holy.

The next six Commandments are about our neighbour.

5. Honour thy father and mother.
6. Thou shalt not kill.
7. Thou shalt not commit adultery.
8. Thou shalt not steal.
9. Thou shalt not bear false witness.
10. Thou shalt not covet.

## The Story of the Good Samaritan

God gave Moses the Ten Commandments on Mount Sinai so that the people would have a rule of behaviour. The first four of the Commandments are about God. The next six are about how we should behave to each other.

Jesus was asked a question: “How shall I live for eternity?” In reply Jesus asked the questioner what it said in the Scriptures. The answer was “Thou shalt love the Lord thy God with all thy heart, all thy soul, all thy strength and all thy mind, and thou shalt love thy neighbour as thyself.” Jesus said “You are right. Do this and you will live in eternity.” The man then said “Well, who is my neighbour?” So Jesus told them a story.

A man was going down from Jericho to Jerusalem, and was attacked by thieves. They stripped him of his clothing and possessions and wounded him, leaving him half dead.

By chance a Temple Priest passed that way. He looked at him, then passed by on the other side of the road. A little while later someone else from the Temple came along the way, but he too passed by without stopping. Later a Samaritan, hated by these Jews, was on a journey. When he saw the injured man he went to him, cleaned his wounds and bound them up. Then he lifted him onto his own donkey and took him to an inn. The Samaritan paid the innkeeper to look after him until he returned from the business he was on. He also said he would pay for any extra expenses.

At the end of the story Jesus asked “Who was the attacked man’s neighbour?” The answer was “The man who showed mercy.” Jesus said “Go and do the same.”
