### The Ten Commandments: Exodus 20:1-12

The first four Commandments were given to Moses by God on Mount Sinai.

1. I am the Lord thy God, thou shalt have no other Gods before me.
2. Thou shalt not make unto thyself any graven image.
3. Thou shalt not take the name of the Lord thy God in vain.
4. Remember the Sabbath day to keep it holy.

The next six concern your neighbour.

5. Honour thy father and mother.
6. Thou shalt not kill.
7. Thou shalt not commit adultery.
8. Thou shalt not steal.
9. Thou shalt not bear false witness.
10. Thou shalt not covet.

## The First Four Commandments are Directly to do with God

**1. I am the Lord thy God. Thou shalt have no other Gods before me.**

God is the only God. There is only one God.

**2. Thou shalt not make unto thyself any graven image.**

Don’t be tempted to treat anything else as being as important as God.

**3. Thou shalt not take the Name of the Lord thy God in vain.**

Don’t be disrespectful in any way to God.

**4. Remember the Sabbath day to keep it holy.**

Keep Sunday as a day especially for God; try to go to church.

**Make God Number 1 in your life.**
