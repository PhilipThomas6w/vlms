## Prayers in Honour of the Mother of God

The hymns we sing to the Mother of God tell of the truths we believe about her relationship with God and her part in our salvation.

### "Axion Estin"

> It is truly meet and right to bless thee, the ever blessed and most pure Virgin and the Mother of our God,\
> More honourable than the Cherubim and beyond compare more glorious than the Seraphim,\
> Thou who in virginity didst bear God the Word,\
> True Mother of God, we magnify thee.

### Theotoke Parthene

> Hail, Mother of God and Virgin, Mary, full of grace.\
> The Lord is with thee; blessed art thou among women,\
> And blessed is the fruit of thy womb, for thou hast given birth to the Saviour of our souls.

### Mary's own song

> My soul magnifies the Lord and my spirit rejoices in God my Saviour...\
> For behold, all generations will call me blessed,\
> For He that is mighty hath done great things for me and Holy is His Name.
