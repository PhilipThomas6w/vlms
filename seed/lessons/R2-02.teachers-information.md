This session is to provide the children with an introduction to martyrdom. Key questions to engage with are:

- What makes someone a martyr?

- Who was persecuting the Christians and why?

To explore these questions, reference to St Stephen and St Polycarp should be made. The letters of St Ignatius of Antioch should also be made. Principally, talk about the language which surrounds martyrdom. It is described as that which enables them to become a golden loaf, as an offering to Christ, who is the bread of life (John 6.35). Notice the other parallels between the life of Christ and the martyrdom of Polycarp. Ultimately, martyrdom is the manifestation of a life truly imitative of the life of Christ, hence they image Him, the bread of life, as a perfectly baked loaf.

- [CHURCH FATHERS: Epistle to the Romans (St. Ignatius) (newadvent.org)](https://www.newadvent.org/fathers/0107.htm) – chapter 4 and 7.

- [CHURCH FATHERS: Martyrdom of Polycarp (newadvent.org)](https://www.newadvent.org/fathers/0102.htm) – especially chapter 15.

Note also the value placed on the relics of these saints, and how they are treated as fine jewels.
