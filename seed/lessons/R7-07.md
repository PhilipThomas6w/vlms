## While They Were Stoning Stephen He Prayed

The book of Acts tells us about the first Christian martyr Stephen, who died seeking to be a “peace-maker”. He was challenging his listeners to turn to God and receive His forgiveness. Like many of the prophets of the Old Testament he was willing to be “obedient unto death” and even pray for his persecutors – that God would forgive them. As he was being stoned Acts tells us, “He saw the heavens opened!”

## Singing the Beatitudes

In some Orthodox Liturgies the Beatitudes are sung during the Procession of the Gospel Book. This is because the Beatitudes are at the heart of Jesus’ teaching.

![A deacon in blue vestments raises the Gospel Book in the Small Entrance, beside a tall candle in a church; the caption reads that the Beatitudes are sung in all Russian Orthodox Liturgies when the Gospel Book is carried in procession](small-entrance.jpg)

## The Beatitudes: A Ladder of/to Love

Saint Gregory of Nyssa said the Beatitudes were a ladder to union with God. We first have to learn what it means to be “poor in spirit” – becoming aware of how much we need God and need to ask to be filled by His Holy Spirit.

![Diagram, The Ladder of the Beatitudes: a ladder with a rung for each Beatitude, from poor in spirit (Matthew 5:3, kingdom of heaven) at the bottom, through mourn, meek, hunger, merciful, pure in heart and peacemakers, to persecuted (Matthew 5:10-12, kingdom of heaven) at the top](ladder-of-the-beatitudes.jpg)

## Meekness Is Strength

It takes courage to be meek; to be gentle and not to react with violence, whether in word or deed – to not want to control everything and everyone!

![A man stands in the road watching a mother duck lead her ducklings across; the caption reads “Blessed are the meek (gentle and patient) for they shall inherit the earth”](meek-and-gentle.jpg)

## A Longing for God’s Presence and Will

“Hungering/thirsting for righteousness” means seeking to “Love the Lord with all your heart and love your… enemy” (Matthew 5:44). You will need to ask to be “filled” with God’s love to be able to do that!

![Picture of a Bible on a plate with a knife and fork; the caption reads “We need to ‘eat’ God’s words (the Bible) to increase our appetite for God. This will help us ‘hunger/thirst for righteousness’. How do we ‘eat’ God’s words?”](eat-gods-words.jpg)

## Confession and the Beatitudes

Going through the Beatitudes is a good way to prepare for Confession. They can help us see where we need to still grow in love. For example: how “pure” is my heart? What do I think, say, or do that makes it impure?
