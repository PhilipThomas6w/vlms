Continuing with the liturgical thread of the previous two sessions, this session aims to introduce the children to saints John Chrysostom and Basil the Great – to whom are attributed Divine Liturgies which are still used in the Church today. St John was introduced in the previous session, with a focus on his description of the liturgy. Teachers are free to develop that theme further if they feel it necessary – otherwise, a look at the *Paschal Homily* should be conducted (an image of the end of it is in the images folder for this lesson). This can then be compared with the Anaphora of St Basil found here: [Anaphora Prayer of St Basil the Great \| Orthocath](https://orthocath.wordpress.com/2010/03/22/anaphora-prayer-of-st-basil-the-great/) . Again, what is it that we’re doing, or joining in with during the Liturgy – nothing short of an ascent into Heaven, into the Eighth Day, the Day to come!
