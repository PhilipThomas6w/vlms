## More about Elias

**Samaria: Ahab’s New Capital.** When Solomon died the Kingdom split. As the Temple was in Jerusalem (the south), Ahab made his capital in Samaria (the north). Here he built a Temple to Baal and Asherah.

**Queen Jezebel: A Nasty Woman.** Jezebel was the daughter of the King of Phoenicia, who worshipped the idol gods Baal and Asherah. She married Ahab, the King of Israel and sadly persuaded him to do the same!

**The Message of Repentance!** Both Elijah (from the O.T.) and John the Baptist (from the N.T.) had the same message – “CHOOSE – You can’t worship the LORD God *and* idol gods.” We have the same choice!

**What’s in a Name?** The name ‘Elijah’ comes from Hebrew and means “The LORD is my God”. In Greek it is ‘Elias’.

**Elijah: Man of Prayer.** “Elijah was a human being, even as we are. He prayed earnestly that it would not rain, and it did not rain on the land for 3+ years.” (Epistle of St James 5 v 17)

**Elijah Confronts Ahab!** It was to Samaria Elijah first came to Ahab to tell him there would be no rain until he stopped the Baal and Asherah worship.

**“God is my Strength”.** Elijah was the Mo Farah of his day! God enabled him to run from Carmel to Jezreel ahead of Ahab in his chariot!

**Meals on Angels’ Wings.** When Elijah was hungry and exhausted after Mt Carmel, and on his way to Mt Sinai, God sent an Angel with food to strengthen/encourage him.

**Ravenous Ravens.** At Cherith God sent ravens with food for Elijah. This must have seemed strange to him as they were thought unclean!

**Jezebel – A Murderess.** Jezebel not only introduced the worship of Baal with its child sacrifice, but killed so many of the true God’s prophets that Elijah thought he was the only one left!

**Baal and Ashtoreth.** The religion of these idol gods was not only useless but evil. Baal was “the rain god” but could produce no rain (or fire). Worse he demanded child sacrifice!

**The Prophet Who Didn’t Die!** Only Elisha saw Elijah ascend into heaven in what looked like a fiery chariot! Enoch is the only other man God just “took” from the earth.

## Stories from His Life

**Raising the Widow’s Son.** This miracle made the widow exclaim, “The LORD is God”, a further miracle as she lived in “Jezebel (Baal) country”.

**Elijah’s Mantle.** Elisha was ploughing when Elijah put his robe on him. At once he left his work and followed Elijah, until Elijah was taken up and he became the next great prophet.

**Give Us … Our Daily Bread.** Because the widow obeyed Elijah and made him bread with her last bit of oil and flour, God gave her more, but only enough for each new day!

**King Ahab & Queen Jezebel.** “Ahab did more evil than any of the kings before him.” “Jezebel tore down God’s altar, killed the priests and replaced them with 450 ‘prophets’ of Baal and 400 of Asherah.” 1 (3) Kings 16

**Elijah Famous Quote.** “And Elijah came to all the people, and said, ‘How long will you falter between two opinions? If the LORD is God, follow Him; but if Baal, follow him.’ But the people answered him not a word.”

**Naboth’s Vineyard.** Naboth refused to sell his vineyard to Ahab who coveted it. Jezebel had Naboth killed! Elijah then prophesied that Jezebel would die a violent death, which indeed she did!

**Who Controls the Heavens?** The prophets of Baal prayed all day and nothing happened. Elijah prayed and fire came down and consumed even the water and 12 altar stones.
