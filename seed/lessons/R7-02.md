## The Service of Vespers

Vespers is an evening service. In some Orthodox churches the time of the service changes so that it takes place at sundown every day.

Vespers is the Christian service that could be said to replace the evening ceremony in the Jewish Temple, when the incense was replaced by the lamp. This followed the instructions given to Moses by God.

There are many Old Testament themes which come through to our Vespers from ancient Jewish worship. This is not surprising because the first Christians were Jews who were used to Temple worship and reading the Scriptures in their synagogues. They simply took what they were used to doing and gradually included Christian themes.

Themes in Vespers: Creation, Light, Hope.
