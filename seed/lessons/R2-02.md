## St Stephen the First Martyr

St Stephen was one of the 7 deacons ordained by the Apostles to help in the Church with the poor, widows and orphans. He was an excellent teacher. But he made the authorities of the Jews very angry by reminding them that they had put Jesus to death, explaining all the Old Testament prophecies that told them that Jesus was in truth the Son of God. The Jews were so angry they took Stephen out of the city and stoned him to death. Stephen saw the Heavens open and he asked God to forgive those who killed him. He is known as the Proto or First Martyr. His feast day is the 27th December.
