One aim of this story driven session is to get the children to see how the holy ones of God live their lives, and how we are to emulate them. In addition to the resources provided, the following video can also be consulted: [Saint Spyridon the Wonderworker and Champion of Orthodoxy (youtube.com)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=rQqI8jwsy_s). There is also a wordsearch with a few questions in the lesson, on St Nicholas.
