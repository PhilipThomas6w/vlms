## The Ten Plagues of Egypt

The story of how God freed the Hebrew people from slavery in Egypt through Moses, and the ten plagues that fell on Egypt before Pharaoh let them go. The passages below are from the Book of Exodus.

![A large number 10 divided into ten coloured sections, one for each plague: water turned to blood, frogs, lice, flies, dead cattle, boils, hail, locusts, darkness and the death of the firstborn, with the title “Plagues of Egypt” beneath](ten-plagues-of-egypt.png)

1. Water turns to blood
2. Frogs
3. Lice
4. Flies
5. Sick cattle
6. Boils
7. Fire hail
8. Locusts
9. Darkness
10. Death of the firstborn
