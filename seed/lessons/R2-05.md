## Receiving Holy Communion

We receive Holy Communion at the Holy Liturgy just as the disciples did at the Last Supper.

Christ blessed the bread and wine at the Last Supper and made it His body and blood.

In the Liturgy the Priest asks the Holy Spirit to come down and make the bread and wine the Body and Blood of Christ.

When you receive Holy Communion you have God within you. This changes you. You become holy.

This is why we are very respectful in church and prepare ourselves carefully.

Before you receive Communion, you should venerate **one** icon to show that you are an Orthodox Christian. Then you should stand with your hands across your chest, ready and waiting. This shows other people that you intend to receive Communion. It also makes sure that arms don’t knock the chalice (cup) or spoon.

If you have to wait a long time, look at the icons on the screen and the candles and think about God coming to you.

(We do not kiss an icon after the Communion has been brought out of the altar, as God is in the Communion, so you don’t need to venerate an icon at this time.)

Before you receive Communion, make the sign of the cross. When it is your turn, hold the red cloth under your chin with two hands so that no Communion ever drops on to the floor. Wipe your mouth and then take a small piece of bread to eat to make sure you swallow all the Communion.

Stand quietly and still while everyone else receives.

***You have God in you!***

## Preparation for Receiving Communion

Before coming to church to receive God in the Holy Communion we should prepare ourselves.

Here are some ways:

- Make a special effort to be clean and tidy.
- Gradually learn to have nothing to eat (start by just having a drink and a piece of toast).
- Say some of the special Communion prayers.
- Fast on Wednesdays and Fridays.
- When old enough, make regular confessions.
