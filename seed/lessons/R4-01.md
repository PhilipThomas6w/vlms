## The Holy Table

The Holy Table is:

- the Table of the Last Supper;
- the empty tomb;
- the Tabernacle, as in the Old Testament.

At the consecration of a church the Holy Table is washed, anointed and dressed as at Baptism.

## To Dress the Holy Table

1. **Relics** are the bones of Martyrs, sometimes from churches destroyed and no longer in Christian countries. They are placed in a hole in the centre of the new Holy Table. Wax mastic is melted and used to seal them in.
2. **Icons of the Evangelists** are fixed onto each corner of the new Holy Table.
3. **The white cloth** is a baptism garment. This is never taken off.
4. **The Holy Table cloth** can be coloured, sometimes red or gold. This can be removed and changed if needed.
5. **On the Holy Table are kept:**
   1. The Gospel Book
   2. The red cloth used for Communion
   3. The blessing cross
   4. The Artophorion (the Holy Gifts are kept in this for the sick)
   5. Candlesticks
   6. The Antimension, the cloth on which Communion is consecrated. This is the Bishop's licence.
6. **Behind the Holy Table:** the crucifix, and the Exapteriga. These are fans showing that Seraphim are surrounding God, as there were in the Ark of the Covenant and later in the Temple in Jerusalem. We hear about this in the Old Testament.
