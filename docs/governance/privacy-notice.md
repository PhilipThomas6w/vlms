## How Varangians uses your and your child's information

**Who we are.** Varangians is the youth club of Shrewsbury Orthodox Church. The church is the data controller for the information in the Varangians app. `[TBC – Philip: legal name and address of the controller]`. Contact us about your data at `[TBC – Philip: contact email]`.

**What we hold and why.**

- **About your child:** name, date of birth, when they joined, the lessons they have completed, their rank, badges and certificates. We use this to run the programme and to show you their progress.
- **Your annual consent form:** whether your child may be photographed, go on trips, and whether their information may be shared. We use this to know what your child may take part in. If the consent form has expired, a teacher cannot record new lessons for your child until it is renewed.
- **Health and emergency information:** emergency medical information, dietary or special educational needs, and an emergency contact. We use this only to keep your child safe at sessions and trips. Only the club's Admin and Safeguarding Officer can see it, and every time they open it, this is recorded.
- **About you:** your name, contact details, the email address you sign in with, and which child you are linked to.
- **Volunteers:** your name, email, role, and your DBS check (date, expiry, certificate number and outcome). Only the Admin and Safeguarding Officer can see DBS records.
- **When anyone uses the app:** sign-in details (your password is stored only in scrambled form), two-factor authentication settings, and a security log of who opened health or DBS records.

For example: running the club is in our legitimate interests; photos and trips rely on your consent, which you can withdraw at any time; health information is processed as part of the church's not-for-profit activities with its members `[or: with your explicit consent]`.

**Who else handles it.** We do not sell or share your information for marketing. It is stored and processed for us by:

- DigitalOcean (our server, in London);
- Cloudflare (encrypted backup copies) `[TBC – Philip: location]`;
- Microsoft (sending emails from the club's address) `[TBC – Philip: location]`.

Each acts only on our instructions. We share information with others only where the law requires it, or to protect a child (for example, telling emergency services about a medical condition). `[TBC – Philip: any other recipients, e.g. the diocese]`

**How long we keep it.**

- Your child's records, including consent and health information: 3 years after they leave the club, then deleted.
- DBS records: 6 years after the check expires or the volunteer leaves.
- The security log of who viewed health or DBS records: 6 years.
- Backup copies are kept for up to `[TBC – Philip: e.g. 35 days]` after that, then overwritten.

**Your rights.** You can ask to see the information we hold, to correct it, or for it to be deleted or restricted, and you can object to how we use it. Where we rely on your consent you can withdraw it at any time. For your child, a parent can usually exercise these rights; older children may be able to do so themselves. Contact `[TBC – Philip]`. We will reply within one month.

**Automated decisions.** The app promotes a student automatically when every lesson in their rank is complete, and it stops new lessons being recorded while consent has expired. These are fixed club rules applied to the records, not profiling, and a teacher or the Admin can correct them.

**Do you have to give us this information?** We need the consent form and emergency information before your child can take part in sessions. `[TBC – Philip: confirm]`

**Complaints.** Please contact us first. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office: [ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint](https://ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint/), 0303 123 1113.
