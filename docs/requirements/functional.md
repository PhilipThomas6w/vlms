# Functional Requirements

Status: in progress

## Curriculum management (MVP)

- Any Teacher can propose a change to a lesson (content edit, new lesson, retire a lesson).
- Only the Approver role can sign off / approve a proposed change before it goes live.
- Review is ad hoc only — no scheduled/termly review prompt; teachers propose changes whenever they spot something worth changing.
- Rejection supports comments and resubmission: the Approver can leave feedback on a rejected proposal, and the Teacher can revise and resubmit the same proposal (not just raise a fresh one).
- Each lesson has a delivery order within its rank and one or more topic tags from a fixed list taken from the programme syllabus: Church Life, Worship, Church Interior, Mysteries, Prayers, Saints, Church History, Old Testament, New Testament, Church Year, Christian Life (Philip's decision, 21/10/2026).
- A lesson can have several content files (a main document plus images and handouts), not a single file (Philip's decision, 21/10/2026).
- The initial curriculum is seeded from `seed/curriculum.json`. Ranks 1–7 have 8 lessons each; Cross-bearer (8), Christ-bearer (9) and Akolouthos (10) are seeded with no lessons, to be filled later. The lesson files are supplied at seed time and are not committed to the repository (Philip's decision, 21/10/2026).
- **Lesson content access (MVP, go-live blocker; Philip, 22/10/2026):** the core purpose of the app. Lesson files are opened in the app or downloaded by:
  - **Teachers:** all lessons in every rank.
  - **Students:** all lessons in every rank, not only their own.
  - **Parents:** all lessons in every rank.
  - **Uploads:** a teacher uploads new or replacement files as part of a lesson change proposal, and they go live only when the Approver approves it, never directly.
  Detailed as FR-006 (library, view and download) and FR-007 (suggestions, review and history) below.
  Discovery (a300c93, 17/07/2026) recorded student access to lesson material as deferred "at-home learning", and the user journeys were never written, so content access was never built. Philip confirms it was the key requirement, and this entry corrects the record.
- **Longer-term aim (post-MVP):** each lesson's content becomes a single printable HTML page, with handouts attached, that users read in the app without downloading anything. This replaces the current Word documents (Philip, 21/10/2026).

## Progress tracking (MVP)

- Auto-promotion: a student is automatically promoted to the next rank once every (active) lesson in their current rank is marked complete.
- Certificates (per lesson completion) and badges (per rank promotion) are real tracked records in the system — not implicit/ad hoc — giving an audit trail of who received what and when.
- The Teacher marks a lesson complete for a student, and can self-correct/reverse their own entry (e.g. within some reasonable window) — no separate correction gate through Admin. An evidence/note field is available but optional (low friction, not a mandatory audit gate).
- Certificates are auto-generated documents (PDF, from a template) at completion time — not just a database record. This resolves the ambiguity in the prior build (which left certificate generation unspecified). Implies a template + document-generation component in design, and storage/delivery (download vs email) — [TBC design].

## Safeguarding & consent (MVP) — confirmed matching prior build's model

Discrete `FR-nnn` numbering assigned here specifically (per design review), given the safeguarding weight of this module — other sections remain narrative pending fuller FR-nnn numbering before build.

- **FR-001:** Annual parental consent record per student covering: photo/media consent, emergency medical info, dietary/SEN needs, transport/off-site trip consent, data-sharing consent. Amended 22/10/2026 (Philip, `adr/0008`): transport (which may be a short trip between sites) and off-site trips are separate consents, and dietary requirements and special educational needs are separate fields, each long enough for a full description. Consent runs to 31 August, the end of the church year. Approved by Safeguarding Officer or Admin only. Parents complete and submit it themselves in the app (FR-015).
- **FR-002:** DBS (Disclosure and Barring Service) check tracking for teachers. Whole-record access restricted to Admin and Safeguarding Officer only — Teacher and Approver have no access (`governance/security-compliance.md`).
- **FR-003:** Expiry monitoring (consent expires on 31 August, `adr/0008`; Admins and Safeguarding Officers are alerted when a consent form is submitted, and linked parents are emailed 30, 14, 7 and 1 days before 31 August, and weekly while a child has no consent in force or a rejected form waits for resubmission, Philip 22/10/2026): consent and DBS expiry are tracked, with escalating alerts, and **expired consent blocks lesson completion** for that student until renewed.
- **FR-004:** A `StudentGuardianLink` (parent-to-child relationship) is created only by Admin/Teacher at student registration — never by parent self-service — before a parent can access any progress, medical, or consent data for that child.
  - Amended 22/10/2026 (Philip, `adr/0008`): parents sign up and register their children, completing consent in the same flow. The registration is an application; the `Student` and `StudentGuardianLink` are created only when an Admin approves it. On approval the Admin places the child in an age-group category, which sets the starting rank (not always the first). Amended again 22/10/2026 (`adr/0008` answers): a Safeguarding Officer reviews first and may ask the parent for more information, then an Admin gives final approval and assigns the starting rank; Admins can also register a child directly; Teachers no longer register children or create guardian links. Phone, relationship to the child, postal address and a completed consent form are required. The same child name under the same parent is refused as a duplicate.
  - Accepting a guardian invite creates the account only; it grants no role. An Admin approves the guardian by granting `Parent` (Philip's decision, 11/10/2026).
  - If the invited email already belongs to an account (for example a Teacher who is also a parent), the invitee signs in to that account to accept, and the existing identity is linked to the guardian record. Ownership is proved by signing in, never by matching the email alone; `Parent` is still granted by an Admin. One identity can hold several roles. (Philip's decision, 22/10/2026.)
- **FR-005:** Every read of a `DbsCheck` record, or of a `ConsentSensitiveDetails` record, is written to an audit log (who, what, when).

## Parent engagement (MVP)

- Parents get both an in-app dashboard (view own child's progress, certificates, badges) AND proactive notifications (e.g. email on lesson completion/rank promotion, consent-expiry reminders) — not dashboard-only.

## Reporting (MVP)

- Core progress reports: rank/completion stats, promotion history.
- At-risk/disengaged student flagging: no lesson completions within 8 weeks, surfaced to Admin for proactive follow-up.

## Fitness-for-purpose requirements (MVP, go-live blockers; Philip, 22/10/2026)

These come from walking the user journeys (`user-journeys.md`) against the built app on 22/10/2026. The MVP had been built and gate-tested at service level, but a user in the named role could not do these things in the app. A requirement here is met only when a user in each named role can complete it in the running app.

- **FR-006 Lesson library.** Teachers, Approvers, Admins, students and parents can browse every lesson in every rank, in delivery order, with tags, and open or download each lesson file. Files open in the browser where it can display them (PDF, images); other types, such as `.docx`, download. Files are served only to signed-in users holding one of those roles, never through a public link. File names (Philip, 25/10/2026): the library and downloads show clean names, not the scanner names the files came with. Format "Name – Description", e.g. "Emperor and Empress – Drawing" for `Emperor and Empress_ Drawing 07102022_095242_000115.jpg`; no file type shown. A rule produces them and writes a list (`seed/file-names.json`) that Philip can correct by hand; the curriculum seed applies the list.
- **FR-007 Lesson suggestions, review and history (Philip, 26/10/2026; `adr/0013`, replacing the file-upload proposal workflow first built).** A Teacher suggests changes to a lesson from the lesson page, editing its text, pictures, title, order and tags in a word-processor-style editor, with a preview and a required reason. A suggestion can be saved as a draft and submitted when ready; a whole lesson can be replaced the same way, and new lessons and retirements are suggestions too. Only one suggestion per lesson is open at a time. An Approver sees each submitted suggestion as tracked changes against the current lesson and approves it or sends it back with a comment. One Approver's approval is enough, and an Approver who is also a Teacher may approve their own suggestion. Every lesson keeps a permanent numbered history of versions, recording who suggested each change, what changed (shown as tracked changes) and who approved it; the converted lesson is version 1. Students and parents see only approved versions. An Approver may instead publish a change directly ("Publish now"), and may replace a whole lesson from a blank page or pasted text ("Replaced in full", shown side by side in the history), while the National Administrator's national setting allows direct publishing; under *Review required* every change goes through review. The version history is kept whichever way a change is published (Philip, 26/10/2026). A change by someone who is not an Approver always goes to review, and every Approver is alerted when a suggestion is submitted. Any Approver may approve it, send it back, or refer it to committee, in which case it needs approval by three different Approvers (Philip, 26/10/2026; not yet built; assumptions in `adr/0013` decision 8).
- **FR-008 Record lesson completion.** A teacher picks a lesson, sees the students, and marks those who completed it in one action. A student blocked by lapsed consent (FR-003) is shown with the reason, not silently skipped. Certificate, promotion and parent email follow as already built. A teacher may record a lesson from any rank, not only the student's current one, so that a student who joined at a higher rank can catch up on the ranks they bypassed (Philip, 23/10/2026).
- **FR-009 Teacher's student list.** Any teacher sees all active students with their current rank and progress within it, with a filter for students assigned to them, and can open a student's record (completions, badges, certificates) (Philip, 22/10/2026).
- **FR-010 Certificate view and download.** Students, their linked parents, teachers and Admins can view a student's certificate PDFs in the app and download them. This settles the delivery mechanism left [TBC] above. The certificate designs will come later (Philip, 22/10/2026); until then the existing generated PDF is used (`governance/raid.md` D-001).
- **FR-011 Student accounts and student page.** A parent creates a sign-in for each linked child: a username and password, with no email address needed (Philip, 22/10/2026). The parent can reset that password. A student has a page showing their rank, completions, badges and certificates, with a link to the lesson library. Only a linked parent who has not left creates or resets a sign-in; an Admin can also reset it. How a sign-in without an email passes the confirmed-account rule without weakening it for adults is recorded in `adr/0007-student-sign-in-accounts.md`. Decided 22/10/2026 (`adr/0007`): student passwords at least 8 characters with relaxed complexity; students stay signed in on their own device; a parent's or Admin's reset clears lockout; Admins can disable and restore a student's sign-in, and leaving does not disable it; sign-ins are parent-created only, because every student has a consenting parent; one parent account serves all of that parent's children.
- **FR-016 Role changes take effect immediately.** A user is one identity with any combination of roles (for example Teacher and Admin, Teacher and Safeguarding Officer, Teacher and Parent); permissions are fixed per role, with no separate permission layer. Granting or removing a role takes effect on the user's next action, including in pages already open (Philip, 22/10/2026). A student account holds only the Student role.
- **FR-017 Leavers keep access.** Marking someone as having left records that the programme no longer needs ongoing records for them (consent renewal, expiry monitoring) and starts the retention clock; it does not lock them out. Leavers are encouraged to keep using the learning materials, and safeguarding records are kept for the legal retention period regardless (Philip, 22/10/2026). Exception (Philip, 22/10/2026): Teachers, Admins and Safeguarding Officers who leave are locked out entirely; they cannot sign in at all. Students and parents who leave keep access. Clarified 23/10/2026 (Philip): a staff member who is also a parent keeps parent access to their own children after leaving, and loses everything their staff roles gave them; an Approver who leaves keeps access (an Admin can remove the role if wanted).
- **FR-018 Resend a guardian invite.** An Admin can resend the account-setup invite to a guardian who has no account yet; the new invite replaces any earlier unused one, so only one invite works at a time (Philip, 22/10/2026).
- **FR-019 No duplicate guardian for an existing account.** When an Admin registers a new guardian (on Register student or Guardian links) with an email that already belongs to an account, the app refuses it and says that an account already exists for that email, pointing the Admin to "Link an existing guardian". No second guardian record is created (Philip, 23/10/2026; found in the second browser walk).
- **FR-020 Attendance register (Philip, 24/10/2026; built 24/10/2026, `docs/design/chapters-and-attendance.md` section 7; rebuilt 24/10/2026 as one register per class per meeting day, FR-022).** Teachers take a register at each session and attendance is tracked over time, per student and per chapter. The register is self-contained in each chapter: only that chapter's staff take and see it. Nationally, attendance is available only as anonymous figures, never names. Parents see their own child's attendance and progress. Marks are present or absent only; a student is flagged at risk after two absences in a row (Philip, 24/10/2026; `docs/design/chapters-and-attendance.md` section 5).
- **FR-021 Chapters (Philip, 24/10/2026; built 24/10/2026, `docs/adr/0010-chapters.md`).** Varangians is to grow into chapters at Orthodox parishes across the country; Shrewsbury Orthodox Church is the first chapter, not the whole system, and the Greek Orthodox Church of the Holy Transfiguration, Coventry, is added as a second chapter for testing. Every student, guardian, consent, DBS record, completion, register and registration application belongs to a chapter, and chapter staff see only their own chapter's people. The curriculum, ranks and badges are national. A national administrator (Philip) sets up chapters and assigns each chapter's Admins; a chapter's Admins appoint its other Admins, Safeguarding Officers and Teachers. Nothing in the app or its text may assume a single parish. Decisions: `docs/design/chapters-and-attendance.md` section 5.
- **FR-022 Calendar (Philip, 24/10/2026; built 24/10/2026).** A calendar showing the Church's liturgical calendar alongside each chapter's own events. Every chapter is under the Archdiocese of Thyateira and Great Britain (Ecumenical Patriarchate) and follows the New Calendar published by the Patriarchate, the same calendar the Greek Orthodox Archdiocese of America publishes as an ICS feed (its Digital Planner); the app imports that feed. Chapters add their own events (the days they meet). Feed (Philip, 24/10/2026): `https://calendar.google.com/calendar/ical/i0foh8u5am8ui8grpo1svvaun4%40group.calendar.google.com/public/basic.ics`, GOARCH's "Orthodox Feasts - English" Google calendar; checked 24/10/2026: downloads without a browser (13 MB), one all-day entry per day from 01/09/2017 to 31/08/2027, the day's main feast as the title and the saints, fast and readings in the description; one calendar across years, so the address should not change each September. Decided (Philip, 24/10/2026): only a chapter's Admins add events; no credit or attribution to GOARCH is shown (not required, and it adds clutter). Decided (Philip, 24/10/2026): attendance is taken at every event, classes included; each class has its own register; a register is opened from its event in the calendar, not from a typed date, so only events an Admin has created can have a register (this replaces FR-020's one register per chapter per meeting date). The calendar shows each day's main feast and its fasting status, with the saints and readings opened from the day. Each person gets a private link to subscribe to their chapter's events in their own calendar app. Events and classes (Philip, 24/10/2026, replacing the same day's earlier answer on class-only absences): an event is a day the chapter meets; all its classes meet that day. Classes are divided by rank, one class per rank, so a student changes class on promotion and there is no separate class membership. Each class has its own register for the event. Teachers are assigned to teach classes, by an Admin (for example, Philip teaches the Akolouthos); a class's teachers normally take its register, but any Teacher in the chapter can take or update any class's register; when classes are joined (for example, a teacher shortage), that teacher takes each joined class's register. The registers are collated so the total attendance for any event day can be seen. Corrected 25/10/2026 (Philip): the sentence recorded here on 24/10/2026, "Every event is a meeting day, so every absence counts towards the at-risk rule", was not Philip's answer and reversed his earlier one. Philip's answer stands: only absences from the student's own class sessions count towards the "two absences in a row" at-risk rule (FR-020); excursions and other non-class events can be booked and do not count. The app as built on 24/10/2026 counted every register absence; fixed 25/10/2026 (only marks from class registers count). Shrewsbury meets on the third Sunday of each month, September to June; extra events are scheduled during the year as needed, on no fixed pattern. Decided (Philip, 24/10/2026): every event is scheduled individually by an Admin, with no repeating pattern; extra meeting days have one register per rank, like any other; on a meeting day a Teacher sees their own classes' registers first and can open any other class's from the same page; an Admin assigns Teachers to classes on the chapter's staff page, and a Teacher can teach several classes and a class can have several Teachers. As built (24/10/2026), where Philip's answers did not say: the calendar is read by everyone holding a role in the chapter (staff, parents, students); an event has a name ("Chapter meeting" by default), optional start and end times and an optional place; a register can be taken on or after the event's day, never before; an event's date cannot change, and it cannot be removed, once a register has been taken at it; a student promoted after their class's register was taken stays on that register for that day; the liturgical calendar is imported daily, after the safeguarding sweep, keeping what it had if the feed cannot be fetched; the subscribe link carries the chapter's events from 12 months ago onwards, and stops working when replaced, stopped, or when its owner no longer belongs to the chapter. List or grid (Philip, 25/10/2026; built 25/10/2026): the month shows as a list of days or as a month grid, switched on the page and remembered per person on every device; until a person chooses, the list shows on phones and the grid on wider screens. On phones the grid shows each day's number, a dot for an event and the fast as a one-word pill; tapping a day opens it below the grid (events, feast, fast, saints and readings). Confirmed (Philip, 25/10/2026): the week starts on Monday; on wider screens the grid also shows event names and the feast. Chapter events and added saints (Philip, 25/10/2026; replaces importing parish calendar feeds, judged too hard after the Shrewsbury feed was analysed on 25/10/2026): no further feeds are subscribed to; GOARCH stays the only imported calendar. The chapter corresponds to the parish. Events are in two categories: Varangians events (a class, an excursion) and parish events (a service, a meeting of some kind). Registers are needed for Varangians events only; only class absences count towards the at-risk rule. An Admin can create recurring events as well as one-off events. A National Administrator can add British and Irish saints (British includes Welsh; Irish added by Philip, 25/10/2026) to the saints of the day, nationally; this adds to the saints only, never the feast, fast or readings. A patronal feast is a parish event, added by the chapter's Admin. Added saints are shown in the day's saints list after those from the GOARCH feed. An added saint is hidden on any day where the GOARCH feed already lists the same name, as GOARCH's coverage varies by year (Philip, 25/10/2026); the comparison is of exact names only, so it never hides a different saint. A saint may be added on more than one date (for example Melangell on 31/01 and 27/05; Philip, 25/10/2026). The National Administrator can also import a list of British and Irish saints from a file such as a .csv (Philip, 25/10/2026). Confirmed (Philip, 25/10/2026): two columns, date (DD/MM, repeating yearly) and name; the import shows a preview first, with each row's errors and that date's GOARCH saints beside it, so duplicates can be left out before anything is saved; rows already added (same date and name) are skipped. A starting list drawn from the Shrewsbury parish calendar, current and past entries, with saints already in the GOARCH feed on the same day left out, is in `seed/british-saints.csv` (25/10/2026; 128 saints, reviewed and approved by Philip 25/10/2026, Urscinus kept). As the chapter's calendar is now generated by the app, there should be a unique .ics subscribe link for it. Confirmed (Philip, 25/10/2026): recurrence is weekly on a chosen day, monthly on the nth weekday (for example the third Sunday), or yearly on a date, each series with an end date, and each date can then be changed or removed on its own; an excursion has one register for everyone on it, not one per class. Corrected 25/10/2026 (Philip): a public chapter link carrying parish events only was Claude's proposal, which Philip confirmed with other drafts; it was built and then removed the same day at Philip's direction. The app is for the chapter's members (parents, teachers, staff), not the public, and they need the Varangians events in their calendars. The chapter's subscribe link is therefore each person's own private link (above), which carries all the chapter's events: classes, excursions and parish events. There is one subscribe panel on the calendar. Event kinds and recurrence, as built (25/10/2026), where Philip's answers did not say: every event has a kind (Class, Excursion, Service, Parish meeting), existing events becoming classes; a chapter has at most one Varangians event (class or excursion) a day, while parish events are not limited; parish events have no register and are not meeting days; excursion marks count nowhere in attendance figures (not the at-risk rule, a student's attendance history, the roster's recent attendance, or the national figures); an event's kind, like its date, cannot change once a register is taken; a repeating event is created as a series of separate events, each changed or removed on its own, up to 5 years ahead; a series is refused whole if any of its dates already has a class or excursion (naming the clashing dates); monthly on the nth weekday skips months without that week (only the fifth can be missing), and yearly from 29 February falls only in leap years; removing "this and later dates" keeps any later date that already has a register, and says how many were kept. Added saints, as built (25/10/2026), where Philip's answers did not say: on `/administration/saints` (National Administrator, with two-factor authentication); a saint is added on a day and month (any day that exists in a leap year; 29 February shows only in leap years), and the same name cannot be added twice on one date; "the same name" ignores letter case and surrounding spaces, for both that rule and hiding a saint the GOARCH feed lists that day; added saints show after the feed's in alphabetical order, on days the feed covers; the import's preview shows that date's GOARCH saints for this year (29 February: the next leap year), and leaves unticked any row whose name the feed already lists that day; a file is at most 256 KB and 2,000 rows, a header row `date,name` is optional, D/M is read as well as DD/MM, and a row repeated in the same file is shown as an error; an added saint can be removed one at a time, after a confirmation. The starting list is imported on the page like any other file.
- **FR-023 Lessons saved for offline use (Philip, 25/10/2026; `adr/0011`).** Anyone who can open the lesson library can save a lesson to their phone, tablet or computer and open it with no internet connection. Saving is by choice, lesson by lesson (the whole library is about 300 MB, too much to save automatically). Nothing else needs to work offline. Many users, teachers included, have very poor technical skills, so this must add as little as possible: one clearly worded button per lesson, no settings, nothing to set up, and when there is no connection the app opens straight onto the saved lessons instead of an error. Decided (Philip, 25/10/2026, confirming the drafts): (1) the button reads "Save to this device", then shows "Saved on this device" with "Remove"; (2) with no connection, opening the app shows "No internet connection" and the list of saved lessons, each opening its files; (3) saved lessons update themselves the next time the app is opened online, and a lesson removed from the library is removed from the device; (4) saved lessons stay on the device after sign-out (lesson content is not personal data, and losing it at sign-out would confuse people); (5) on an iPhone not yet added to the home screen, the button shows one line: "To keep saved lessons, add this app to your home screen", linked to plain step-by-step help; (6) Word documents open in the phone's Word or Google Docs app, as they do online now. Amended after the build (Philip, 25/10/2026): decision 5 is one line at the top of the lesson library, not one per lesson, and the save buttons stay hidden there until the app is on the home screen; the button shows "Saving…" while it saves and "Could not save. Try again." if it fails; the help page has a fourth step, "Open the app from its new icon on your home screen". Interim: met in the PWA now; the .NET MAUI app stays on the roadmap.
- **FR-024 App-hosted lessons (Philip, 25/10/2026; `adr/0012`).** Lessons are read in the app, not downloaded: each lesson's Word documents and pictures are converted once into lesson text with its pictures, shown on a lesson page that works on a phone and can be saved for offline use (FR-023, which it amends: a saved lesson is that page and its pictures, with no separate offline rendering, and the "Save to this device" button is on the lesson page as well as the library; Philip, 26/10/2026). Text in scanned pages (prayers, services, recipes, instructions) is written into the lesson text, with citations where possible and appropriate, and Philip reviews each converted lesson. Each rank's Teacher's Information is a section at the top of the rank's lessons, shown only to Teachers and Admins. Original photographs stay on the server; a resized copy is shown and saved to devices. The app is not yet released, so there is no change-over period: a rank's original files leave the lesson library once it is converted and reviewed. Herald is converted first as a pilot. Editing afterwards is through lesson suggestions (FR-007, `adr/0013`).

- **FR-013 Student details.** A student's name, date of birth and other personal details come from the parent's consent form (FR-015): an approved consent submission updates the student record. Only Admins assign a student's teacher and change a student's status (Philip, 22/10/2026).
- **FR-014 Rank badge images.** An Admin uploads one badge image per rank, shown wherever badges are listed.
- **FR-015 Parent consent form.** A parent completes the annual consent form (all FR-001 fields) for each linked child in the app and submits it. A Safeguarding Officer or Admin approves it or rejects it with comments, and the parent sees the status and can resubmit. Access to `ConsentSensitiveDetails` (Philip, 22/10/2026): linked parents can view and update their own child's details; teachers can view them; Admin and Safeguarding Officer as before. This amends ADR-0004, recorded in `adr/0006-parent-consent-and-teacher-read-access.md`. Every read stays audit-logged. `DbsCheck` stays Admin and Safeguarding Officer only. The DPIA must be updated, because parents enter, and teachers read, special-category data.
