Rank badges: the proposed collection

For approval before anything in the application changes. Ten badges, Recruit to Akolouthos, drawn as vector art. Each one is shown large, then inside the real rank medallion at the sizes the application uses (the badge fills 76% of the medallion and is clipped to a circle), faded and greyed as the application shows ranks in progress and not yet earned, and on the page colours of the student interface: paper #F7F8FA, white panels, the rank tint behind the current station, and the lapis bar #14254F. The medallion rings use the proposed rank colours (one row shows today's for comparison).

Rank by rank

Rank 1 of 10

Recruit

The eight-pointed star that led the Magi to Christ: the first light on the way, and the beginning of a journey made together.

Rank 2 of 10

Guardsman

The helmet of salvation (Ephesians 6:17): a guard's conical helmet with the Cross on its brow, for vigilance and service.

Rank 3 of 10

Shield-bearer

The shield of faith (Ephesians 6:16): the kite shield of the Guard's own day, bearing the Cross.

Rank 4 of 10

Sword-bearer

The sword of the Spirit, which is the word of God (Ephesians 6:17): an upright sword over the open Gospel.

Rank 5 of 10

Herald

The herald's trumpet with the pennant of the Cross: the Faith proclaimed and carried outwards.

Rank 6 of 10

Torch-bearer

The server's processional candle: the light of Christ that enlightens all.

Rank 7 of 10

Standard-bearer

The labarum, the standard of the Cross bearing the Chi-Rho, carried before the army of Constantine and in the Church's processions.

Rank 8 of 10

Cross-bearer

The processional cross with IC XC NI KA, Jesus Christ conquers: the Cross at the centre of the procession and of the faith.

Rank 9 of 10

Christ-bearer

The chalice of Holy Communion marked with the Cross and IC XC: receiving Christ, the communicant carries Him into the world.

Rank 10 of 10

Akolouthos

The double-headed eagle, emblem of the Orthodox Church, for the commander of the Guard who stood closest to the Emperor.
